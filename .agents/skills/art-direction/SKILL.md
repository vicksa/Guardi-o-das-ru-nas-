# Art direction

Create a sculptural low-poly fantasy world: blue basalt ruins, jade vegetation, luminous amber magic and an immense warm moon, suspended over atmospheric clouds. Make the 3D world fill the screen. Use expressive silhouettes: a small cloaked mage with a crooked staff and a massive stone guardian with a glowing core. Use elegant cream serif display type and restrained mono labels. Draw original 3D geometry, no franchise characters, emoji placeholders or generic dashboard grids. Keep HUD compact and hand preview unobtrusive. Mobile overlays must leave controls reachable, camera must remain visible during calibration, and menus must scroll when needed.
