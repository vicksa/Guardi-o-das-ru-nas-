# Art direction

Create a sculptural low-poly fantasy world: blue basalt ruins, jade vegetation, luminous amber magic and a deep atmospheric night sky, suspended over atmospheric clouds. Make the 3D world fill the screen. Use expressive silhouettes: a small cloaked mage with a crooked staff and a massive stone guardian with a glowing core. Use elegant cream serif display type and restrained mono labels. Use detailed stylized fantasy characters with rich PBR materials. Licensed CC0 character models are permitted with provenance; draw original environment and guardian geometry. Avoid franchise characters, emoji placeholders or generic dashboard grids. Keep HUD compact and hand preview unobtrusive. Mobile overlays must leave controls reachable, camera must remain visible during calibration, and menus must scroll when needed.
