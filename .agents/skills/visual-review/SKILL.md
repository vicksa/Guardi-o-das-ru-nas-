# Visual review

Inspect screenshots of title, gameplay, controls, pause, camera loading/failure, victory, defeat, desktop, portrait mobile and landscape. Check all primary actions remain visible, HUD is legible, the mage contrasts with terrain, telegraphed attacks and puzzle targets are visible, and the 3D canvas is not cropped. Exercise camera startup, cancel, compatible mode, retry preserving stream, keyboard and simultaneous touch. Record evidence and limits in docs/VALIDATION.md. Do not claim physical hardware camera verification.
